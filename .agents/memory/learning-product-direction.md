---
name: Learning product direction
description: User-specified experience boundaries and approval requirement for Lifewise design changes.
---

The user wants Lifewise to take strong inspiration from Duolingo's learning
experience, including rewards and streaks, while retaining Lifewise's identity.
They explicitly requested removing mentor, traces and proof features/tabs.
Do not reintroduce those learner modules as part of future visual refreshes.
They also require reviewing and approving the design before design changes
are pushed to GitHub.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for a learning/rewards-focused redesign
and withheld GitHub-push approval until they could review the design.

**How to apply:** Keep learner-facing experiences focused on life-skills
learning and practice, and show real earned progress rather than fabricated
gamification. Preview changes in the workspace, ask for design approval, and
do not interpret implementation requests as approval to push the new design.
